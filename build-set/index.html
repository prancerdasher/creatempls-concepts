We bring the lab to the kids, not the other way around.

createMPLS is a Minneapolis nonprofit that runs hands-on STEM inside the places students already spend their day: classrooms during school, and after-school, summer and community programs around them.

Students build robots, code games, print parts, record sounds and fly drones. They test what they made, change one thing, and show it to someone. Partners provide the room. We bring the rest.

  • AFree for every student and family, by design.
  • BDelivered on-site, in school and out of school.
  • CReal tools, real mentors, real things made.
Fig. 2 — robotics practice, 2016
Building since 2015

It started with one robotics team and a cafeteria table.

Ten years on, the kit is bigger: coding, fabrication, circuits, sound, flight and design. The idea is the same. Show up where kids are, hand them real tools, and let them build.

STEP2

Snap it together.

Every session follows the same build rhythm. Short demo, long build, then test it, fix it, and show it off.

1

See it once

~5 MIN

A quick demo, then hands off. Nobody sits through a lecture.

2

Build sprint

~20 MIN

Teams get the most time on the clock, with real parts in their hands.

3

Test it

MEASURE

Does it hold? Does it run? The test tells you, not the teacher.

4

Change one thing

~15 MIN

Second sprint. Fix the link that failed and run it again.

5

Show it off

~10 MIN

Every build ends in a share: a demo, a gallery walk, a race, a listening party.

ASSEMBLY TIP

We never cut the last ten minutes. That's when kids explain what they built, and it's often the best part.

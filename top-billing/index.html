How it works · it comes to you

The show comes to you.

We bring the instructors, the kits, and a ready-to-run program into your building, during the school day or after it. For a few weeks your room becomes the studio.

Students make something real in the first session, test it, change one thing, explain it, and close with a show. Students and families pay nothing.

It comes to us!A createMPLS parent
Act I

Make

Hands on parts in the first ten minutes. Short demo, long build sprint, real tools.

Act II

Test

Run it, watch it fail, change one thing, run it again. That loop is the lesson.

Act III

Show

Every program ends with a share: a show night, a listening party, a tournament, a gallery walk.

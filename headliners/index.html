How it works

The show comes to you.

We bring the instructors, the kits, and the plan into your school or community site, during the school day, after school, or in summer. Students make something real, test it, change one thing, and show it to the people they care about.

Students and families pay nothing. No bus, no tuition, no gear to buy.

Parent or caregiver? Here's how to bring it to your kid's school.

Act I

Make

A short demo, then a long build sprint with real tools. Hands on parts in the first ten minutes.

Build sprintReal tools
Act II

Test

Run it. Watch it fail. Change one thing. Run it again. That loop is the lesson.

Try · fix · retryOne change
Act III

Show

Every program ends with a share: a showcase, a demo, a race, a listening party. Kids explain how it works.

    How funding works

    1. First call: you tell us your grades, dates, and space. We tell you what it costs your site, and for many sites that's $0.
    2. We look for coverage first: createMPLS grants and local sponsors.
    3. If there's a gap: the usual sources are Title I, 21st CCLC (after school), or PTO and site budgets. We'll write the program description for your request.
